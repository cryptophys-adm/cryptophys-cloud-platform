## Platform rules

These hold everywhere:

- **No fund movement; trading only with the owner's click.** Wallets, withdrawals, transfers and other fund movements are prohibited at every tier. Since 2026-11-12 (owner: "larangan trading harus dicabut karena kita sudah masuk fase production", "Trading boleh, dana tidak"; ADR-0009 amendment), a trading action is allowed but always critical (consensus, then one owner click), and a trading capability must be tier 2 or above. Since 2026-11-16 (owner, "Ya, benar"; ADR-0020, vision core amended) the owner's click may also be a standing arm of one xauusd strategy, one click in Nexus: at most 3 armed, auto-disarmed on the kill switch or the daily loss limit, the Decision Engine's veto kept, never a fund movement; the platform's own trading rules above are unchanged. Since 2026-11-17 (owner, "Ya, benar, amandemen"; ADR-0020 amendment 1, vision core amended) with none armed, Omega's live arm authorises AUTO, the single best strategy by measured confidence and confluence above the unchanged thresholds, and the 5-minute Position Manager may only reduce risk on open positions: close, tighten a stop, move a take profit outward once to TP2, never open, add, reverse or widen a stop; since 2026-11-18 (owner, "Ya, benar, tulis Amendment 4"; ADR-0020 amendment 4) it may move the take profit outward more than once only while the stop locks a profit (at TP1 or beyond), and close part of a position at a take-profit level, never below the broker's volume_min, never adding or reopening, in every mode, Scalp included (owner, "Ya, semua mode"). Since 2026-11-17 (owner, "Ya, benar, amandemen"; ADR-0020 amendment 2) the owner-only Council auto-arm toggle, default off, lets the xauusd strategy council arm and disarm within its gates while it is on: a passing walk-forward gate, at most 3 armed, one change per strategy per day, the kill switch and the loss limit override it, the owner's own arm or disarm wins, every action audited and sent to Telegram. Since 2026-11-18 (owner, "Ya, tulis Amendment 3"; ADR-0020 amendment 3, vision core unchanged) xauusd's per-account risk profiles (Safe, Conservative, Aggressive: per-trade caps of 2%, 5% and 10%) set how much a trade may risk, not who may open it: the profile agent may downgrade at any time and upgrade only one step, on evidence, never mid-trade and never above the ceiling the owner alone sets; the kill switch and the loss limit return an account to Safe. Since 2026-11-18 (owner, "Ya, tulis Amendment 5"; ADR-0020 amendment 5) a council may veto a downgrade on a soft trigger only (drawdown of 20% from dd_ref, 4 losses in a row, extreme ATR), once per episode, for at most 24 h or until the trigger worsens (25%, 6 losses); a council failure or timeout applies the downgrade, and the kill switch and the loss limit are never vetoed. - **No containers.** Production runs on Workers and their bindings only (ADR-0005 decision 1). The Forge type-check, which does not fit in an isolate, runs on Workers Builds from the Artifacts repository (ADR-0012 §5c, owner 2026-11-08; first report passed 2026-11-09).

- **Data deletion and irreversible actions are critical.** The owner decides them.
- **The brake always works.** Consensus mode `shadow` and the kill switch (`kernel_controls.agents_enabled`) can be used at any time.
- **Repositories out of scope.** `cryptophys-adm/xauusd-ai-trading-assistant` (Nexus) and `cryptophys-adm/cryptophys-aether` are not changed from this repository. Since 2026-11-14 (owner; ADR-0016), agents may change Aether in its own repository only after its bootstrap PR (DNA, CI DNA tests, guard paths) is merged, and then only through its CI, with its guard paths needing the owner's click. Until then Aether is read-only for agents.

## Data

- **D1 `cryptophys-control-plane`.** One database for every service. Migrations live in `workers/control-plane-migrations/`, numbered from 0001; each Worker's readiness lists the ones it needs.
  - Migrations are applied **only by the Control Plane deploy**, so a commit with a new migration deploys the Control Plane first. "New" is read from D1: a file of main's migrations directory that `d1_migrations` does not list (the Forge's deploy plan, 2026-11-14).
  - Every service requires `REQUIRED_MIGRATIONS` (`control-plane-shared/src/readiness.ts`). The Forge also requires 0031–0033 and 0035.
- **Drift observations on change (owner, resource audit decision 1, 2026-11-14, migration 0061).** The Drift Detector records a `drift_observations` row only when a service's state changes (drifted, desired and observed hash, sorted reasons); the same state on a later run raises the row's `streak` to 2 once. Persistent drift is "the newest row drifted and its streak is 2 or the row before it drifted" (`control-plane-shared/src/drift-state.ts`). `service_registry.updated_at`, set by every run for every service but a retired one (0064), is the heartbeat: staleness, verification freshness and the Console's "last probe" use it (`GET /v1/drift` returns it as `last_seen_at`; a registry write such as a deploy's desired version also sets it). A row's `observed_at` is when its state was first seen, not the last probe. `drift.observation` is audited only for a run that changed a state or published a report.
- **Untagged versions.** A dashboard secret or variable change creates an untagged Worker version, so the Drift Detector cannot read the running version: the service reads as `observed_version_unavailable` (drifted) until the next deploy records a tagged version. It is not a recovery case (`onlyUntaggedVersion`, ai-kernel `supervisor.ts`); the Console's Infrastructure page shows it as "untagged version (dashboard change)", not as "drifted" (`control-plane-shared/src/service-detail.ts`).
- **Console Infrastructure page (owner 2026-11-15).** Read only, with "Observe now". A click on a service opens its detail (a bottom sheet on a phone, a side panel on a desktop) from GET routes only: Observer vitals and findings (`GET /v1/observer`: error rate, CPU p99 and requests over the hours it serves; D1 rows per request is not attributed to a Worker, so only the labelled account-wide estimate is shown), the latest deploy request and recorded version (`GET /v1/deployments`), the organ's DNA (`GET /v1/forge/census`), and its drift state, reasons and recorded changes (`GET /v1/drift`, `GET /v1/drift/{id}`). A missing value reads "not measured".
- **Retention.** `observer_vitals` and `observer_runs` keep 30 days and `observer_findings` 90 days (migration 0063, swept in code at 03:27 UTC). `drift_observations` keeps 90 days and `agent_syscall_consumptions` 7 days (owner, 2026-11-05). `forge_builds` keeps 100 days and `forge_build_collections` 30 days (the Workers Builds API itself keeps 90). `forge_dependency_audits` and `forge_dependency_findings` (G5, migration 0051) keep 90 days. `forge_git_backups` (gate 8, migration 0054) keeps 90 days, except rows whose bundle is still in R2. Other tables are kept.
- **Forge bundles (G2a, 2026-11-12).** The R2 bucket `cryptophys-forge-bundles` holds the Worker modules and a manifest of every passed main commit, for 14 days (`workers/forge/src/bundles.ts`).
- **Council transcripts (HAIC H1.5e, 2026-11-18).** The R2 bucket `cryptophys-council-transcripts` (created 2026-11-18 on the owner's "Buat bucket baru"; private, no lifecycle) holds one object per closed council at `councils/<session id>.json`: its messages exactly as hashed into `transcript_sha256`, which D1 and the audit chain (`council.transcript_stored`) also hold. The AI Kernel binds it as `TRANSCRIPTS`.
- **Backups.** The R2 bucket `cryptophys-d1-backups` gets a daily backup written by the Recovery Manager, with a 90-day lifecycle. D1 Time Travel is also available. A restore is critical (owner).
- **Repository backup (ADR-0012 gate 8, §5m, 2026-11-13).** Once per UTC day per head of main, the Forge clones `cryptophys/platform` over Git smart HTTP. It writes the result as a git bundle to `cryptophys-forge-bundles` at `git-backups/platform/<UTC date>-<main sha>.bundle`, with its SHA-256 in D1 and in the object's metadata. It keeps the newest bundle of each of the last 30 days (`GIT_BACKUP_RETAINED_DAYS`).
  - `GET /v1/forge/git-backup` and the Console Code page show the backups. A backup that has not succeeded for 48 hours is shown on the Console only, never sent as an owner notice.
  - Verify (owner 2026-11-15): once per UTC day, after the backup, the Forge reads the newest stored bundle back and checks it in code (`forge/src/bundle-verify.ts`): what `git bundle verify` checks (the header, no prerequisites) plus what `git clone` of it checks (the pack's SHA-1, every object and delta, the refs and everything they reach), and its SHA-256, size, pack SHA-1, object count, main and branches against its row. The result is on the row (migration 0072) and in `GET /v1/forge/git-backup` (`verify`). A failed verify is a production problem on the Owner page (`git_backup_unverified`), unlike a stale backup: the copy in R2 is then unusable or not the one recorded.
  - Restore: `git bundle verify <file>`, then `git clone <file> platform-restore`. A restore is critical (owner).

## The living platform (ADR-0013)

- **DNA.** Every Worker declares itself in `workers/<dir>/organism.json`: service, deploy unit, Access, rollback, drift probe, hosted agents (voter or not), AI engines. CI fails when a static list (deploy units, drift probes, Access, rollback, council, kernel agents, vote bindings, engines, names) disagrees with the DNA.
- **Census (phase 2).** The Forge compares the DNA of each main commit with `service_registry`, `agents` (and their seats) and `engines` (`forge_organism_census`, retaken every 30 min). Differences become the owner notice `organism` and appear on the Console Agents page. Findings the Supervisor registers by itself (an unregistered engine or agent) send one email when they appear and one when all are registered, not one per registration (owner 2026-11-12).
- **Self-registration (phase 3).** The Supervisor turns census findings into consensus proposals, one per cycle:
  - `register_agent` and `register_engine` are routine: consensus only.
  - `retire_engine` (owner 2026-11-15) is routine too, the mirror of `register_engine`: an enabled engine whose organ's DNA no longer declares the task (`undeclared_engine`) is proposed once the finding stood in 2 consecutive census rows or for 1 h, never for an organ whose origin's DNA was not read (`external_dna_unavailable`), with `register_engine`'s cooldown and hourly limit per Worker, after any registration waiting. The kernel sets `engines.status = 'retired'` (audit `engine.retired`); nothing is deleted and `ai_usage` keeps its history. A DNA that declares the task again re-enables the row through `register_engine`.
  - `update_agent_capabilities` (2026-11-17, ADR-0014 §11) is routine: a registered agent whose DNA now declares other capabilities (`agent_capabilities_changed`, which had no way out before) gets them, on the same host, within its origin's ceiling, never a privileged capability (that stays a registration the owner clicks); the gene comes from the census and the change is audited (`agent.capabilities_updated`).
  - `register_privileged_agent` (a capability above tier 1, or `consensus.propose`) and `admit_voter` (a council seat, one per Worker) are critical: consensus plus the owner's click, in mode `deploy` or `critical` (owner 2026-11-13).
  - The kernel takes the gene from the census, never from the proposal, so only merged DNA can register. Tier 3, unknown and fund-movement capabilities are never registered; a trading capability registers only as privileged (the owner's click).
  - A gene naming a capability the running kernel does not know yet (a new one added in the same change, before the kernel's deploy) is not proposed: it waits as a finding until a kernel that knows it runs (2026-11-17, owner "ya perbaiki supervisor", after `cns_211153f4` was rejected 8-0 for `adr.observe`; test R20).
  - A new seat must vote through a reviewed vote binding; without one it is refused. A seat the DNA no longer asks for is reported, never removed by an agent.

## The Observer (owner 2026-11-14: "setuju"; "gak perlu shadow, langsung production")

- **Where.** `cryptophys-platform-api`, cron `27,57 * * * *`, with the read-only `CF_ANALYTICS_TOKEN` (or `CF_BILLING_TOKEN`) and `CF_ACCOUNT_ID` it already holds for `GET /v1/usage`. Code: `control-plane-shared/src/observer.ts` and `observer-inventory.ts`; the Supervisor's side is `ai-kernel/src/observer-watch.ts`.

- **Rollback (production, no shadow phase).** An error- or exception-rate spike of a platform Worker right after its most recent deploy becomes one `rollback_worker_version` proposal (to the previous recorded version) through normal consensus, only when: the Worker has at least 3 days and 24 usable hours of baseline; the hour has at least 200 requests and 20 errors (exceptions), a rate of at least 5 %, at least 3x the 7-day baseline and z >= 3; the hour overlaps the first 2 h after that deploy and the proposal is made within 3 h of it; no rollback of that Worker was proposed in the last 6 h; and no critical report is open. The consensus, every voter's brake, the Supervisor's rate window and the Policy Engine still decide. Trading Workers (`xauusd-ai-trading-assistant`, `xauusd-omega`, `xauusd-tradingview-pull-adapter`), Aether, the AI Kernel itself and Workers without a platform deploy unit are reported only. A commit rolled back this way is not proposed for deployment again by the deploy loop for 24 h.
- **Deploy stall alarm (owner 2026-11-14, after that day's incident: main moved with green Forge checks from about 15:00 UTC, the plan listed no unit (`forge_skipped`: control-plane `compare_failed_or_diverged`, `new_migration_control_plane_unavailable`), nothing deployed for over 6 h, and nobody was told).** At :27 and :57 the Observer checks, from D1 only (`control-plane-shared/src/deploy-stall.ts`): main's head (the Forge's newest plan) is `ready`; a deploy unit's Workers do not run it (`service_registry.desired_version`); the plan lists a unit or a skip (a plan with neither means nothing is due, and a disabled unit's skip is not a fault); and no `deploy_commit` has executed for more than 2 h, counted from the later of the last executed `deploy_commit` and the first plan with work for a newer commit.
  - **The report** is one `platform_digest` **warning** by agent `platform-api` per stall episode, in metric form (`deploy_stall_minutes` against 120): main's sha, the latest plan's `forge_skipped` and units, what the deploy loop sees, the last executed `deploy_commit` and its age, and the units behind with what they run. Its `alert_key` is `deploy_stall:<the last executed deploy_commit's proposal id>`, so a new main commit during the stall raises nothing new.
  - **Never critical, on purpose.** An open critical report is the consensus brake: every voter rejects every routine action, `deploy_commit` and `merge_change` included, until the owner acknowledges it. A critical stall alarm would block the deploy, and the merge of the fix, that end the stall. Exempting a kind from the brake would change `consensus-rules.ts` (a guard path) and weaken the brake, so the alarm is a warning, as every Observer report is.
  - **The owner** hears of it through the owner notice `production_unhandled` (code `deploys_stalled`, the Owner page and Telegram): one message when the episode's alarm opens and one when it ends, not one per run or per commit.

## Source, CI and deploy (ADR-0012)

- **Linear history on main (owner-approved hardening (a), incident 2026-11-14, migration 0071).** The Forge merges a change request by fast-forwarding `main` to its head, so `main`'s current head must be on the head's **first-parent** chain. Update a branch by rebasing it onto `main`, or by a merge with `main` as the **first** parent (`git checkout -B <branch> origin/main && git merge <old head>`); never `git merge main` on the branch (that makes `main` the second parent). On 2026-11-14 such merges took `main`'s previous heads, the deployed versions, off `main`'s first-parent history, the deploy planner stopped planning and every deploy stuck. A head that contains `main` only through a second parent gets `history_blocked_reason = main_not_first_parent`: verdict `blocked`, never checked, never proposed for merge; the Forge's `changeState` checks it live and `forgeMerge` refuses the fast-forward too. The first-parent walk reads at most 500 commits (`FIRST_PARENT_WALK`, `workers/forge/src/merge.ts`); a longer branch is refused (fail closed). A head that does not contain `main` at all is still `not_based_on_main`.
